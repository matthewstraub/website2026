About

MS

Obsessed with the intersection of tech & people

I spend my time where the product meets the person using it. Most recently that meant customer success for the Limitless Pendant, an AI wearable, on contract through Meta's acquisition of the company. Before that, years as a Mac and systems engineer running device fleets for agencies, media companies, and schools.

The through line is that I keep ending up as the person who translates. Customers to engineering, engineering to customers, and increasingly, whatever I need into a tool that did not exist that morning.

My background is anything but traditional. I built my own B.A. in Interdisciplinary Studies with a concentration in Critical Audio Design at Marymount Manhattan College, blending audio, music, and a liberal arts foundation into something uniquely my own. I still teach undergrads as an adjunct, and I co-organize localhost:3000, an AI builder meetup in NYC.

Originally from a small town in Northeastern Pennsylvania. I bring small-town curiosity to big-city problems, and I usually go looking for the angle nobody else checked.

Surviving to Thriving

Students scan a QR code, pick an emoji, and rate themselves 1 to 10, from barely surviving to absolutely thriving. The room average updates live over WebSockets, and outlier detection quietly flags anyone drifting away from the group.

It started as a weekend build to answer a question I could not ask out loud in a full classroom. I use it in my own courses every semester, which means it gets debugged by the least forgiving user base there is.
